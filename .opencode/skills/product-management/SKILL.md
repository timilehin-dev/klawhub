# Product Management (v1.1.1)

Expert product management workflows: competitive briefs, metrics reviews, product brainstorming, roadmap updates, sprint planning, stakeholder updates, research synthesis, and spec writing.

## Commands

### /product-management:competitive-brief
Create competitive intelligence briefs. Analyzes: product overview, target market, business model, key features & differentiators, pricing, user experience, strengths & weaknesses, and strategic implications. Includes market positioning maps.

### /product-management:metrics-review
Review product and business metrics. Analyzes: adoption (DAU/MAU, signups, activation rate), engagement (session duration, retention cohorts, feature usage), revenue (ARR, MRR, LTV, ARPU, churn), and health (NPS, CSAT, CES, support tickets). Generates trend analysis, insights, and recommendations.

### /product-management:product-brainstorming
Facilitate structured brainstorming sessions. Methods: SCAMPER, How Might We, Crazy Eights, Round Robin, Reverse Brainstorming, Mind Mapping. Produces prioritized idea list with impact/effort assessment.

### /product-management:roadmap-update
Draft roadmap communications. Sections: vision & strategy, key themes, now/next/later breakdown by theme, key dates, risks & dependencies, ask from stakeholders. Formats for exec summary presentation.

### /product-management:sprint-planning
Plan and manage sprints. Flows: 1) Review sprint goal, 2) Capacity planning, 3) Story selection & sizing (T-shirt or points), 4) Task breakdown, 5) Acceptance criteria definition. Outputs sprint plan with capacity allocation.

### /product-management:stakeholder-update
Craft stakeholder communication. Format: TL;DR, wins, key metrics, risks, asks, timeline. Filterable by audience (executive, team, cross-functional, customer). Humor and emoji optional per audience.

### /product-management:synthesize-research
Synthesize product research into actionable insights. Input: user research, market data, analytics, support tickets, competitive intel. Output: key findings, themes, user needs & pain points, opportunity areas, recommendations. Logs to `research-sessions/`.

### /product-management:write-spec
Write product specifications (PRDs). Sections: 1) Problem Statement & Background, 2) Goals & Success Metrics (OKRs, KPIs, target values), 3) User Personas, 4) User Stories & Acceptance Criteria, 5) Functional Requirements, 6) Non-functional Requirements, 7) Design & UX (links to mockups/prototypes), 8) Technical Considerations, 9) Dependencies, 10) Launch Checklist. Customizable depth.

## Workflows

- **Research competitors**: `/product-management:competitive-brief` on a competitor
- **Review metrics**: `/product-management:metrics-review` with data
- **Brainstorm ideas**: `/product-management:product-brainstorming` on a problem
- **Write a spec**: `/product-management:write-spec` for a new feature
- **Plan a sprint**: `/product-management:sprint-planning` with backlog
- **Synthesize research**: `/product-management:synthesize-research` with raw data
- **Update stakeholders**: `/product-management:stakeholder-update` on progress
- **Draft roadmap**: `/product-management:roadmap-update` with strategic context
